🛡 Villager Safety Instructions
🚪

Stay Indoors

When a Critical alert is active, do not step outside after dusk. Keep doors and windows closed.

🐄

Secure Livestock

Bring all cattle, goats, and sheep into enclosed sheds immediately. Do not leave animals unattended.

🔦

Use Light & Noise

Carry a bright torch if you must go outside. Make noise while walking. Lions avoid loud, visible people.

👥

Move in Groups

Never go to fields or forests alone during active alerts. Travel in groups of at least 4–5 people.

📞

Emergency Helpline

Call 1800-233-7878 (toll-free, 24×7) if you spot a lion or leopard. Do not approach alone.

✅

Wait for All-Clear

Resume normal activities only after receiving an official All-Clear from GirGuard AI or Range Officer.
